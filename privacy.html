PocketPDF Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 16, 2026 · Last updated: September 16, 2026

PocketPDF is an offline document scanner and PDF creation app provided by Demin.yin (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This policy explains how the app handles your information and what happens if you contact us for support.

The current version does not require an account and does not upload your scans, document contents, or app preferences to developer-operated servers.

1. Information processed on your device

PocketPDF accesses and stores the information needed to provide the features you use:

  • Document images: photos you take in the app and images you select for import, including any information contained in those files.
  • Draft information: document names, page order, creation and modification dates, file references, and saved editing information.
  • Generated files: PDFs and temporary files created when preparing, saving, opening, or sharing a document.
  • Preferences: your language, appearance, export-quality settings, and whether you have completed the introduction.

This information is used locally to display and organize your documents, restore drafts, remember settings, and generate PDFs. Original imported files may contain embedded metadata; importing a file is not an anonymization service.

2. Permissions and device access

  • Camera: requested when you choose to scan a document. It is used to capture document photos. Audio recording is disabled.
  • Photos: images are imported through the system picker when you select them. The app does not scan your entire photo library or request broad media-library access on Android.
  • Saving files: the system file picker lets you choose a destination for an exported PDF. The Android app does not request broad storage permissions.

PocketPDF does not request microphone, contacts, or device-location permissions. You can deny or revoke camera access in your device settings; camera scanning will then be unavailable, while photo import remains available.

3. Collection, advertising, and analytics

The app does not send document contents, app-usage events, advertising identifiers, or device identifiers to us. It contains no advertising, third-party analytics, or third-party crash-reporting SDKs. The current version has no in-app purchases and does not collect payment information.

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Your operating system and app store may process information independently under their own privacy policies and your device settings.

4. Saving, opening, and sharing documents

When you choose to save, open, or share a PDF, PocketPDF passes the file to the location, viewer, or app you select through the system interface. A cloud drive, email app, messaging service, or other recipient may upload, store, or otherwise process that file under its own privacy policy.

These transfers are initiated by you. Check the destination and document contents before sharing. We cannot retrieve or delete copies held by another app, service, or recipient.

5. Storage, retention, and deletion

Drafts, imported image copies, and preferences remain in the app’s private storage until you remove them or the app’s data is removed. There is no automatic expiry period for drafts.

  • Deleting an app document removes its draft information and associated original and processed images from the app’s private document folder. It does not delete the source image in your photo library.
  • Temporary exported PDFs are stored separately and may remain even after a draft is deleted. On Android, clearing the app’s storage removes these files along with other app data.
  • Uninstalling or deleting the app normally removes its private local data. On iOS, offloading the app can retain its data; deleting it is different from offloading it.
  • Externally saved or shared copies remain in their destination until you delete them there. Deleting a draft or uninstalling PocketPDF does not remove those copies.

The Android app is configured to disable automatic app-data backup and exclude its data from Android’s standard cloud-backup and device-transfer mechanisms. On iOS, system backups may include app data depending on your device settings. PocketPDF does not provide its own cloud backup or synchronization service.

Export important PDFs before clearing app data or uninstalling. We do not hold a server copy and cannot recover deleted local documents. There is no account to delete because PocketPDF does not create user accounts.

6. Security

PocketPDF stores working documents in app-private storage and relies on the operating system’s app isolation and device security protections. It does not provide a separate encrypted vault or password protection for exported PDFs.

Protect your device with a screen lock and keep its software updated. Files you export are subject to the security of the destination you choose. No device or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Information you send to support

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you voluntarily include. We use that information to respond to your request and resolve the issue. It may be processed by the email providers involved in delivering and storing the message.

Please avoid sending sensitive document contents unless needed for your request. Support correspondence is retained only as long as needed to handle the request and meet applicable legal obligations. You may ask us to delete your support correspondence using the contact below, subject to any retention required by law. We cannot access or delete documents stored only on your device.

8. Children’s privacy

PocketPDF does not ask users to provide an age or date of birth, and its local-processing approach applies to all users. If you believe a child has sent us personal information through support, contact us so we can review the request and delete that information where appropriate.

9. This policy page

This page includes no analytics scripts, tracking pixels, cookies, or externally hosted fonts. When accessed online, the hosting provider may process basic request information, such as an IP address and browser details, to deliver and secure the page under its own privacy policy. Visiting this page does not give us access to documents in the app.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app or its data practices change. We will publish the revised policy and update the date above. If a change requires additional notice or consent, we will provide it as required before applying that change.
