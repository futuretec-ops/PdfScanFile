Delete your PocketPDF account and associated data

PocketPDF is provided by Demin.yin. You can delete your account in the app or request deletion by email.

Delete in the app

Open PocketPDF → Settings → Pro subscription → Delete app account. Sign in again when prompted and confirm deletion.

Request deletion without the app

Email us from the Google email address used for your PocketPDF account. Use the subject “PocketPDF account deletion request”. We may ask you to verify ownership before deleting the account.

Request account deletion

If the email button does not open, send the request to kevin_ydm@163.com.

What is deleted

  • Your PocketPDF Firebase Authentication account.
  • Account-linked Pro entitlement, subscription status, verification timestamps, and our private purchase-token associations.

What is not deleted

  • Scans, OCR text, drafts, and PDF files stored only on your device. Delete them in PocketPDF or clear the app's storage.
  • Files saved or shared outside PocketPDF. Delete those copies from their destination.
  • Transaction records retained by Google under Google Play policies or legal obligations.

Deleting your PocketPDF account does not cancel an active Google Play subscription. Cancel it separately in Google Play subscription management.

In-app deletion normally begins immediately. Email requests are completed after identity verification and within 30 days. If legal or security obligations require us to retain limited information, we will explain the reason and retention period.

PocketPDF Privacy Policy

删除 PocketPDF 账号及关联数据

PocketPDF 由 Demin.yin 提供。您可以在应用内删除账号，也可以通过电子邮件提交删除申请。

在应用内删除

打开 PocketPDF → 设置 → Pro 订阅 → 删除应用账号，根据提示重新登录并确认删除。

不使用应用提交删除申请

请使用注册 PocketPDF 账号时使用的 Google 邮箱发送邮件，主题填写“PocketPDF 账号删除申请”。为防止他人冒用，我们可能要求您验证账号所有权。

提交账号删除申请

如按钮无法打开邮件应用，请发送至 kevin_ydm@163.com。

将删除的数据

  • PocketPDF Firebase Authentication 账号。
  • 账号关联的 Pro 权益、订阅状态、验单时间及我们保存的购买凭据关联记录。

不会由此删除的数据

  • 仅保存在设备上的扫描件、OCR 文字、草稿和 PDF 文件。请在 PocketPDF 中删除或清除应用存储。
  • 已保存或分享至 PocketPDF 之外的文件副本。请前往相应目标位置删除。
  • Google 依据 Google Play 政策或法律义务保留的交易记录。

删除 PocketPDF 账号不会取消有效的 Google Play 订阅。请另行前往 Google Play 订阅管理取消订阅。

应用内删除通常会立即开始。邮件申请将在身份核验后 30 天内完成。如因法律或安全义务必须保留有限信息，我们会说明原因和保留期限。
